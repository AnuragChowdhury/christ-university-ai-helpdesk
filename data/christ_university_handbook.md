## Section 2: Attendance Policies and Regulations
- **Minimum Mandatory Attendance**: Students must maintain a minimum of 85% aggregate attendance across all courses in every semester/trimester to be eligible for End Semester Examinations (ESE).
- **Condonation / Medical Margin**: Students having attendance between 75% and 84% due to medical conditions, institutional representation, or bereavement may apply for condonation. Applications must be submitted along with verifiable medical certificates, prescription receipts, and hospital discharge summaries to the Office of Attendance within 3 working days of returning to campus.
- **Strict Ineligibility (<75%)**: Students having aggregate attendance below 75% are strictly ineligible to appear for the End Semester Examinations. They will receive an 'SA' (Shortage of Attendance) grade and must repeat the course or attend special remedial sessions during vacation terms as per Academic Council guidelines.
- **Grace Attendance**: Granted strictly for verified inter-collegiate fests, sports events, NCC, NSS, and official University representations after prior approval from the Dean/Director.

## Section 3: Examination and Grading System
- **Evaluation Weightage Structure**:
  - Continuous Internal Assessment (CIA): 50% or 70% depending on program structure (CIA 1: 10 marks/assignment, CIA 2: Mid-Semester Exam 25 marks, CIA 3: 10 marks project/case study, Class Participation & Attendance: 5 marks).
  - End Semester Examination (ESE): 50% or 30%.
- **Minimum Passing Criteria**: A student must secure a minimum of 40% in CIA aggregate and 40% in the End Semester Examination separately, with an overall aggregate of 40% (undergraduate) or 50% (postgraduate - MBA/MS AI & ML/MSc).
- **Grading Scale**:
  - 90% and above: O (Outstanding) - Grade Point 10.0
  - 80% - 89.9%: A+ (Excellent) - Grade Point 9.0
  - 70% - 79.9%: A (Very Good) - Grade Point 8.0
  - 60% - 69.9%: B+ (Good) - Grade Point 7.0
  - 55% - 59.9%: B (Above Average) - Grade Point 6.0
  - 50% - 54.9%: C (Average) - Grade Point 5.0 (Passing grade for PG)
  - 40% - 49.9%: P (Pass) - Grade Point 4.0 (Passing grade for UG)
  - Below 40%: F (Fail) - Grade Point 0.0

## Section 4: Fees, Scholarship, and Financial Aid
- **Tuition Fee Payment**: Fees must be paid exclusively through the student portal (KP - Knowledge Pro) via Net Banking, Debit/Credit Card, or NEFT/RTGS before the announced due date. Late payment incurs a daily penalty of Rs. 100 up to 7 days, after which student portal access may be suspended.
- **Merit Scholarships**: Awarded to top rank holders of each program/class based on academic performance in the preceding year, covering up to 20-50% of tuition fees.
- **Financial Support / Fee Concession**: Available for economically weaker students upon submission of verified family income certificates and review by the University Welfare Committee.

## Section 6: Dress Code, Code of Conduct, and Anti-Ragging
- **Dress Code for Male Students**: Formal shirts tucked in, formal trousers, and formal leather/canvas shoes. Groomed hair and clean-shaven or neat beard. Round-neck T-shirts, ripped jeans, and flip-flops are prohibited. On designated Western Formal days, ties/blazers are required.
- **Dress Code for Female Students**: Formal Salwar Kameez with dupatta pinned, formal ethnic wear, or formal western trousers with formal shirts/blouses. Sleeveless, short tops, and casual slippers are strictly prohibited.
- **ID Card Requirement**: Wearing the Smart ID Card with lanyard around the neck is strictly mandatory at all entry gates, corridors, and classrooms. Loss of ID card must be reported immediately to the Office of Admissions; duplicate fee is Rs. 500.
- **Anti-Ragging Zero Tolerance Policy**: Ragging is strictly banned on and off-campus under UGC Regulations. Any student found engaging in or abetting ragging faces immediate suspension, expulsion, and FIR filing with local police authorities. National Anti-Ragging Helpline: 1800-180-5522. Christ Anti-Ragging Cell contact: antiragging@christuniversity.in.
